1. Forensic Clause-by-Clause Scrutiny

FATAL TRAP: Unrestricted Right of Alienation & Sale (Para 3, Page 2) STRIKE-THROUGH REQUIRED
"The Attorney is hereby empowered to enter into contracts, execute sale agreements, accept Bayana, execute registered sale deeds, receive total sale consideration, and surrender peaceful physical possession to any purchaser whatsoever on behalf of the Principal."

Senior Counsel Finding: Under Section 85 of the Qanun-e-Shahadat Order, 1984, the Sub-Registrar and Revenue Patwari rely strictly on the four corners of this document. If this clause is attested at the London Embassy, your attorney acquires full legal authority to transfer the property to himself, his nominees, or land syndicates without your further written consent.

MANDATORY SUBSTITUTE CLAUSE (Chambers Standard 4B)
"The appointed attorney shall have authority SOLELY AND EXCLUSIVELY to oversee physical maintenance, supervise tenancies, and discharge government utility charges. Under NO circumstances does this instrument confer any right of sale, conveyance, gift (Hiba), mortgage, charge, exchange, or surrender of possession. Any purported transaction violating this negative covenant shall be null, void ab initio, and criminally fraudulent under Pakistan Penal Code Section 420/468."

Statutory Shield: This negative covenant deprives the attorney of ostensible authority and defeats subsequent bona fide purchaser defenses under Section 41 of the Transfer of Property Act, 1882.

LATENT TRAP: Right of Substitution & Delegation (Para 6)

The draft permits the attorney to appoint a substitute. In Pakistani property disputes, corrupt relatives use this clause to assign powers to predatory litigation cartels who then register adverse possession claims. This clause MUST be struck out entirely.
