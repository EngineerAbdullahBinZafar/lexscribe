Critical Overseas Legal Advisory

Before You Sign Any Document at the Embassy or Consulate:

Over 70% of overseas property fraud originates with a General Power of Attorney (Mukhtar Nama Aam) drafted by a relative or local agent. If your document fails to explicitly bar the power of sale, gift (Hiba), and mortgage, your land can be liquidated legally without your knowledge.

1. The Unrestricted Power of Sale (Ikhtiyar-e-Baye)

Vague boilerplate phrases like "deal with property in any manner" authorize your agent to sell ancestral land and pocket the proceeds.

2. The Hidden Right of Gift (Hiba bil Ewaz)

Corrupt agents use gift clauses to transfer land to their spouses or children, evading sale consideration and tax trails.

3. Sub-Delegation to Land Syndicates

Permits the attorney to appoint rogue third-party agents who cannot be located once the fraud is executed.

Can an attorney in Pakistan sell my property if I gave them a Power of Attorney?

Only if the Power of Attorney specifically grants the power of sale (Ikhtiyar-e-Baye). However, unscrupulous parties often smuggle vague general clauses or forge revocations.
