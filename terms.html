Chambers Engagement Terms & Retainer Protocols

Governing Asynchronous Advisory, Forensic Document Scrutiny, and Representation Standards for Overseas Pakistanis.

1. The Asynchronous Written Scrutiny & Advisory Model

LexScribe Chambers operates on a deliberate Asynchronous Written Scrutiny model tailored for overseas Pakistani citizens across diverse global time zones (UK, North America, Middle East, Europe). You submit your digital document scans via WhatsApp or our secure Priority Digital Intake Desk. Senior Counsel analyzes the papers in chambers and delivers an authoritative, written Stamped PDF Risk Assessment Report with an accompanying voice memorandum.

This protocol ensures that every legal finding is committed to writing, fully cited against Supreme Court and High Court precedents, and free from the scheduling hassles and distractions of live calls.

2. Absolute Attorney-Client Privilege & Data Confidentiality

Under Section 126 of the Qanun-e-Shahadat Order, 1984, all communications, documents, title deeds, and case briefs submitted to LexScribe Chambers are protected by absolute statutory attorney-client privilege.

No document, deed scan, or personal detail is ever shared with third parties, opposing litigants, or government agencies without your express written instruction.

3. Fee Transparency & Remittance Guarantee

Our fees for Priority Advisory Consultations ($10), Document Audits ($29 / $69), and Written Legal Opinions ($99) are fixed, transparent, and comprehensive. There are zero hidden disbursement fees or surprise administrative charges.

We facilitate direct international payments via Wise, Remitly, Ace Money Transfer, or direct bank wire directly into our verified business accounts in Pakistan, with instantaneous electronic receipts issued upon settlement.

4. In-Court Representation & Vakalatnama

Engagement for forensic document scrutiny does not automatically establish full trial court retainer representation. In-court appearance before the High Court, District Courts, or Revenue Tribunals requires execution of a formal Vakalatnama and agreement on litigation retainer scope.

Senior Counsel Ch. Muhammad Zafar Iqbal directs all trial pleadings and appellate filings, supported by designated junior associates at the relevant District Bar.

5. Pakistan Bar Council Ethical Standards

LexScribe Chambers operates in strict compliance with the Legal Practitioners and Bar Councils Act, 1973, and the canons of professional ethics framed thereunder. Information provided on this platform is for legal education, forensic document scrutiny, and independent advisory.
